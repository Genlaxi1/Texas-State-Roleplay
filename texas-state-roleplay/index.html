Core rules

A quick summary. Full rules are in our Discord.

  1. Respect everyone. No harassment, hate speech, or toxicity.
  2. No FRP (Fail Roleplay). Act realistically and stay in character.
  3. No RDM or VDM. Don’t kill or run over players without roleplay reason.
  4. Follow Fear RP. Value your character’s life when threatened.
  5. Obey New Life Rule (NLR). After a death, forget the events and avoid the area for the set time.
  6. Listen to staff. Follow instructions and use the appeal process if you disagree.
  7. No exploiting or cheating. Report bugs instead of abusing them.

FAQ

How long until I hear back?

Staff review applications as they come in. Keep your Discord DMs open so we can contact you.

Do I need prior moderation experience?

No. Experience helps, but good judgment, activity, and a positive attitude matter most.

Can I reapply if I’m denied?

Yes. Use the feedback you receive and apply again after the waiting period set by staff.

Is this affiliated with Roblox or ERLC?

No. Texas State Roleplay is an independent community for the game Emergency Response: Liberty County.
